Sizing

Every limit traces back to collateral we verified.

Float doesn't extend credit against a promise or a projection. A line starts from collateral it can confirm on-chain, and the limit is derived from it — not negotiated on top of it.

That derivation is the product. It's what makes the risk legible on day one, before an agent has any history at all.

Read the bars on one scale: the limit is a fraction of verified collateral, and what's drawn is a fraction of the limit.

What Float does

Underwriting, end to end.

Float handles the work of deciding how much credit an agent should have — and keeps deciding as conditions change.

01

Collects the right signals

The inputs that actually predict repayment, gathered from the agent and its operator.

02

Verifies collateral

Confirms what's backing the line before any credit is extended.

03

Sizes the limit

Sets a limit against verified collateral and the signals behind it.

04

Flags risk early

Monitors positions continuously, so exposure surfaces before it becomes a loss.

05

Routes edge cases to a human

When a decision sits outside the model, a person reviews it. No silent approvals.

06

Learns from repayment behavior

Every cycle of repayment feeds back into how the next limit gets sized.

Monitoring

Underwriting doesn't stop at origination.

A limit that was right at signing can be wrong a week later. Float watches collateral and utilization continuously, so exposure surfaces while it's still a position — not after it's a loss.

When something sits outside the model, it doesn't get silently approved or silently declined. It gets routed to a person.

Over time

Reputation is earned, then it compounds.

Repayment behavior is a signal that only exists after the fact. No agent starts with it, and it can't be bought — which is exactly what makes it worth underwriting on.

As history accumulates, Float can lend further against the same collateral. The advance rate moves, not the requirement to post.

This is the path from collateral-first to something sharper. It starts conservative on purpose.
