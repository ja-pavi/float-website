Why it matters

Agents can transact. They can't be underwritten.

An agent has no balance sheet and no track record to price. Credit stalls on that decision.

How it works

Start secured. Graduate from there.

A secured card's path to better terms, with on-chain collateral and a verifiable record.

01

Post collateral

Verified on-chain before any credit is extended.

02

Draw on a secured line

Float extends a line matching the collateral, one to one.

03

Build history

Every repayment on time strengthens the record.

04

Graduate

A higher line against the same collateral, or the same line against less. In time, part of the exposure moves to unsecured.

Edge cases route to a person. No silent approvals.
