What Float does

Underwriting, end to end.

Float handles the work of deciding how much credit an agent should have — and keeps deciding as conditions change.

01

Collects the right signals

The inputs that actually predict repayment, gathered from the agent and its operator.

02

Verifies collateral

Confirms what's backing the line before any credit is extended.

03

Sizes the limit

Sets a limit against verified collateral and the signals behind it.

04

Flags risk early

Monitors positions continuously, so exposure surfaces before it becomes a loss.

05

Routes edge cases to a human

When a decision sits outside the model, a person reviews it. No silent approvals.

06

Learns from repayment behavior

Every cycle of repayment feeds back into how the next limit gets sized.

Our approach

Collateral first. Sharper over time.

Float starts where the risk is knowable and earns its way toward everything else. Reputation isn't assumed — it accumulates.

Collateral-first

Credit is backed by verified collateral. Limits are sized against what Float can confirm, not what an agent claims.

  • Collateral verified before a line opens
  • Limits sized to confirmed backing
  • Positions monitored continuously
  • Edge cases reviewed by a human
As history accumulates

Reputation compounds

Repayment behavior is a signal that only exists after it's earned. As agents build history with Float, underwriting gets sharper and limits reflect a track record.

  • Repayment history feeds the model
  • Underwriting sharpens per agent
  • Track record informs future limits
Why Float

A narrow wedge, on purpose.

Credit for agents is an underwriting problem before it's a product problem. That's the part Float is building.

01

Collateral first

Float doesn't extend credit on optimism. V1 is backed by verified collateral, which makes the risk legible from day one.

02

Reputation grows over time

Track record is earned, not granted. As repayment history accumulates, underwriting gets sharper and limits follow.

03

Built for agents and crypto-native operators

Designed for how agents actually transact — not a traditional credit product with an API bolted onto the side.

04

Underwriting is the wedge

Not a broad fintech platform. Float is building the layer that decides who gets credit, how much, and on what terms.
