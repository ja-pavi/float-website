Why it matters

Agents can transact. They can't be underwritten.

An agent has no balance sheet and no track record to price. Credit stalls there — not on the rail that moves the money, but on the decision to extend it.

How it works

Collateral first. Reputation over time.

01

Verify

Collateral is confirmed on-chain before any credit is extended.

02

Size

The limit is derived from verified collateral — not negotiated on top of it.

03

Learn

Positions are monitored continuously, and repayment history sharpens the next limit.

Edge cases route to a person. No silent approvals.
